Iron Jaw — Abilities

1. Projectile & Melee Damage Immunity

Iron Jaw has 100% immunity to projectile and melee damage.

Conventional ranged and close-range attacks are unable to inflict damage on its body.

2. Regeneration Signals

Iron Jaw possesses a powerful regenerative ability that allows its body to recover from damage.

The regeneration is accompanied by unusual signals generated by the entity.

3. Teleportation

Iron Jaw can teleport from one location to another.

This ability allows it to rapidly close distances, escape danger, or reposition itself during combat.

4. Extreme Speed

Iron Jaw possesses extremely high movement speed despite its enormous size.

Its speed allows it to pursue targets and rapidly change its position.

5. Extremely High Intelligence

Iron Jaw possesses an extremely high level of intelligence.

It can analyze situations, understand its surroundings, and adapt its actions accordingly.

6. High Stamina

Iron Jaw possesses very high stamina, allowing it to remain active for extended periods without significant exhaustion.

7. Human Intention Detection

Iron Jaw can detect and understand the intentions of humans.

This ability allows it to distinguish hostile intentions from non-hostile behavior.

8. Natural Weapons

Iron Jaw's body itself functions as a weapon.

Its primary natural weapons are:

- Iron Jaw — its primary attack weapon.
- Claws — sharp claws that emerge from its fists and are used for close-range combat.
