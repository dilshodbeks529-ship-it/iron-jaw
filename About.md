Iron Jaw — About

Appearance

Iron Jaw is a large humanoid entity standing approximately 3 meters tall.

Its arms and legs are approximately 1.2 meters long, giving it a powerful and imposing appearance.

The most distinctive feature of Iron Jaw is its enormous iron jaw, which is permanently attached to its body. The jaw serves as one of its primary natural weapons.

Iron Jaw also possesses sharp claws that can emerge from its fists, allowing it to attack and tear through targets at close range.

Attack Behavior

Iron Jaw primarily attacks by targeting the head of its victim.

Its iron jaw is its main weapon, while its claws provide an additional close-range attack method.

When there are five or fewer targets, Iron Jaw can maintain its focus on its intended targets.

When there are more than five targets, Iron Jaw's targeting becomes less focused and its attacks may be directed toward random targets.

Natural Weapons

Iron Jaw

The iron jaw is Iron Jaw's primary weapon. It is capable of delivering extremely powerful attacks and is an integral part of the entity's physical structure.

Claws

Iron Jaw can produce sharp claws from its fists. These claws are used for close-range attacks and can be combined with its iron jaw during combat.
