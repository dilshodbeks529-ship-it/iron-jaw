One night, a poor middle-aged man who was unmarried was walking alone when five employees suddenly captured him.

When he woke up, he found himself inside a laboratory called John’s Art, owned by a man named John Harvey. Although the name sounded ordinary, terrible experiments were being conducted there.

The man was tied up and taken to a strange capsule. Confused and frightened, he asked:

“What happened to me?!”

One of the employees simply replied:

“Good luck.”

Then he activated the capsule.

At first, everything seemed to be working normally. But suddenly, a technical malfunction occurred, and the entire laboratory began filling with fog. A strange cracking sound echoed through the facility.

Then a long arm suddenly grabbed one of the employees and pulled him away.

A creature appeared at incredible speed and began attacking the people inside the laboratory. Everyone tried to escape, but the creature was too fast.

The horrifying truth was revealed:

The creature was the man who had been placed inside the capsule. He had transformed into Iron Jaw.

The laboratory alarm sounded. The soldiers prepared their weapons. John Harvey loaded a powerful sleeping drug into his weapon and tried to stop Iron Jaw.

The soldiers opened fire.

But the bullets could not penetrate Iron Jaw's body.

Even John Harvey's sleeping-drug projectile failed to affect him.

Iron Jaw moved toward John Harvey at incredible speed and killed him.

Everyone was shocked and terrified.

Iron Jaw then emitted strange signals. These signals caused the blood inside the humans' bodies to come out through their mouths, killing everyone inside the laboratory.

Iron Jaw then left the laboratory.

Three days later, twelve experienced employees whom John Harvey had called arrived at the facility. They were horrified by what they found.

The original plan had been to wait for the twelve experienced employees before starting the experiment. However, John Harvey had started the experiment too early.

Soon, police officers, detectives, and doctors arrived to investigate the laboratory.

Surprisingly, one employee was still alive.

He was placed inside an ambulance, but he died from extreme fear before he could fully explain what had happened.

His final broken words were:

“I... I... It's a tank don't turn to defeat...”

The entire area was evacuated and investigated.

Search teams were sent into the surrounding forest, but they never returned.

Eventually, someone discovered a disturbing scene. A person's shoulder was stuck into the ground, and a wooden board had been placed in his hand.

Something had been carved into the board:

“To kill your Groups.”

It became clear that Iron Jaw was watching the people searching for him.

The closed area was later placed under the command of Major Steve. General Sam ordered Steve to investigate the strange incident at John’s Art.

Steve found the carved message and immediately fainted.

Two hours later, he woke up and sent General Sam a message, telling him not to send any more auxiliary forces or other people into the forest.

Later, Steve suggested observing the forest from a helicopter.

The helicopter entered the area.

It never returned.

Deep inside the forest, a very poor family lived together: a father, a mother, and their daughter.

Iron Jaw did not harm them.

Whenever they saw him, they treated him kindly and gave him food.

Iron Jaw could immediately sense that they had good and clean hearts.

A highly trained soldier named Bill had learned how to move extremely fast. He asked Steve for permission to enter the forest.

Steve refused at first.

After a long argument, Steve reluctantly allowed Bill to go.

Bill secretly followed Iron Jaw.

He eventually discovered a house belonging to the poor family.

Bill was shocked by what he saw. Iron Jaw was not attacking them.

Bill managed to leave the forest alive.

Steve and Bill could not stop thinking about one question:

“Why isn't he doing anything to them?”

Steve decided to test Iron Jaw.

They tied a rope to a cow and sent it into the forest.

Two days later, the cow returned unharmed and even healthier.

Steve then sent two people into the forest.

One was genuinely poor.

The other was a soldier disguised as a poor person.

The genuinely poor person returned.

The disguised soldier never came back.

Later, Iron Jaw attacked the military camp.

Almost everyone was killed.

Bill and Steve managed to hide inside a bunker.

The next day, they came outside and saw what had happened.

Some parts of the soldiers were missing.

They thought they had no choice but to burn the area, but the fire did not work.

General Sam eventually ordered an aircraft to drop a bomb on the forest.

The survivors were evacuated.

The bomb was dropped.

But it did not explode.

Iron Jaw had caught the bomb and safely placed it on the ground.

The next day, he threw it with incredible accuracy.

The bomb landed at General Sam's camp.

General Sam was badly injured.

Seventeen days later, General Sam died.

After him, Julian became the new General.

Julian entered the forest with a large group of soldiers and successfully evacuated the poor family.

Julian also escaped alive.

However, approximately 85% of the people who entered the operation died.

After the operation, Julian was sentenced to death by the court.

After Julian, another man named Allen became General.

Allen entered the forest without weapons.

Eventually, he came face-to-face with Iron Jaw.

Iron Jaw asked:

“Why did you come?”

Allen replied:

“I don't want to contradict you.”

Iron Jaw sensed Allen's true intention and realized that he was being honest.

Then Iron Jaw said something that shocked Allen:

“I want to be human again.”

Allen was stunned.

“What?”

Allen then asked:

“A... Are you human?”

Iron Jaw replied:

“I used to be a human, not now.”

Iron Jaw then told Allen:

“Get out of this forest, and as soon as you leave this forest, you'll forget you talked to me, you survived the resistance, and you'll say you need to get out of here.”

Allen refused:

“No, we are help you!”

Iron Jaw answered:

“No. Just go!”

Iron Jaw then controlled Allen's mind and forced him to leave the forest.

Allen survived.

After everyone had left, Iron Jaw slept peacefully.

One night, he tried to enter the laboratory again.

There, he saw a girl carrying very expensive and delicious food.

The food was actually meant for her dying mother.

The girl wanted her mother to eat expensive and delicious food so that her soul could be at peace.

Iron Jaw understood her situation.

He teleported the girl back home.

Iron Jaw then used his intelligence to prepare a special mixture for himself.

However, his hand was too large to drink it.

The girl eventually came back and saw Iron Jaw.

Iron Jaw looked at her and said:

“Help me.”

He showed her the container and opened his mouth.

The girl helped him.

She thanked him and returned to her mother.

Soon, her mother's condition began to improve.

The girl believed it was simply luck.

But the truth was that Iron Jaw had sent Regeneration Signals to her mother.

Then something incredible happened.

Iron Jaw began transforming.

The terrifying creature slowly transformed back into a human being.

The girl immediately turned around.

The young man covered himself with thick cloth.

The girl then took him home and gave him clothes that had belonged to her father.

For the first time since the experiment at John’s Art, Iron Jaw was human again.
